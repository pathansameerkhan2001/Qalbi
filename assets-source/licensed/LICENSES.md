# Third-party ingredient photographs

QALBI has no photograph of these three botanicals, so these public-domain (CC0)
photographs from Wikimedia Commons are used for the ingredient circles. CC0 means no
attribution is required; the record below is kept so the source can always be traced.
`scripts/process-licensed-images.py` crops them into `public/assets/ingredients/`.

They show the raw botanical only. No packaging, brand or label appears in any crop.
They are **pending store-owner approval**. If QALBI later shoots its own photos,
replace them in /admin/ingredients.
